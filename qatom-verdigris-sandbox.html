How to decide

Verdigris is ownable because it never needs white text.

It reads on light, on dark, and as ink on a filled block. That flexibility is exactly what a signature colour needs. Next step: lock one tone, then decide how far the fill goes on marketing versus the restraint it keeps on technical surfaces.

Identity

What the brand is

The connective layer for agentic commerce.

Qatom is the turnkey, consumer-friendly agentic commerce and verification infrastructure layer, built on the IP and deeptech research from TODAQ Labs.

It is an all-in-one infrastructure layer, not a payment gateway and not a speculative blockchain. It integrates seamlessly between existing AI agents such as Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT and custom LLMs, and the API microservices they call.

What it does

Autonomous agents and human consumers discover, call, pay for, and monetize digital services directly inside plain conversational loops.

Sub-second micropayments settle in-flight by embedding Base64 USD-TDN bearer files into a standard HTTP header, X-TODA-Pay, at a 0% platform take rate.

Every call generates an immutable, tamper-proof decision trace, a TODA Binder, using G-up supportive guild rigging proofs at GET /binder.

Positioning

The category to own

Consumer-friendly agentic infrastructure.

Combine TODAQ Labs academic rigor with Qatom 1-click usability. Position the stack as a transparent layer that weaves between existing software, the plainspoken commercial face of a scientific breakthrough.

The one clarification that matters

Sequence versus simultaneity. Early diagrams drew a multi-step pipeline, Authorize then Execute then Pay.

The real differentiator is simultaneous in-flight finality. Payment, execution and receipt land together in a single HTTP call. Never illustrate a left-to-right, Stripe-style deferred settlement flow.

# Qatom — brand brief for AI agents

Load this before generating anything Qatom-branded (pitch deck, social post, web element, icon, email, doc). Companion machine-readable file: `brand-spec.json`. Visual anchor: **qatom.ai**.

## Assets — one repository
This guide, the spec files, and every canonical asset live in one versioned Git repository — the single source of truth for both people and agents. Read `brand-for-ai.md` + `brand-spec.json` for the rules, then fetch assets by path via the manifest `assets.json`.
- Repo: **`github.com/qatom/brand`** — `/logo` · `/imagery` · `/spec` · `/fonts`. *(PLACEHOLDER — confirm the repo URL and access before publishing.)*
- Never retype the wordmark or redraw the mark: pull the supplied vector from `/logo`.

## The one test
Before shipping, check: **cool neutral ground · exactly one verdigris accent · the type trio · the mark untouched · the Qatom voice.** If all five hold, it's on brand.

## Positioning
- Hero line: **"Turn your APIs into payable agentic storefronts."**
- Descriptor: the all-in-one **sovereign commerce platform for AI** — a headless agentic market and payment rail.
- Tagline: **"Integrity, built into every asset."** Support: *Not promised — proven. Not trusted — verified. Not dependent — portable. Making humans stronger.*
- Master brand: **Qatom** leads everything commercial; **TODAQ Labs** appears only as the endorsement "Powered by TODAQ Labs Core IP."

## Colour (neutral-first, cool, NO BEIGE)
Cool neutrals carry every surface; verdigris is the **accent**, not a field.
- Grounds: near-black `#181818` / `#101010`, cool near-white `#F4F8F6`, mist `#EAF4EF`, oxide `#1C4B4B`, pine `#0C1A17`.
- Signature accent: verdigris `#33BAA6` (deep `#278A7F` for small text on light; bright `#79D2C5` on dark).
- Text: ink `#1C1C1C` on light; near-white on dark.
- **Never**: beige anywhere in a background; a whole surface flooded with saturated teal/green; the same hue stacked on itself; white running text on verdigris (that contrast is logo-strength only).
- The palette is sampled from **sky and water** — fintech conscious of planet and people.
- Verdigris **is** allowed as a *logo* background: white-mark-on-verdigris is the primary favicon / social avatar, plus one hero moment.

## Typography
- **Source Serif 4** — headlines & statements (tracking ~ −0.015em).
- **Hanken Grotesk** — body, UI, money (15–19px body, line-height ~1.6; tabular figures).
- **IBM Plex Mono** — eyebrows (11px UPPERCASE, 0.2em tracking), labels, figures, identifiers.
- **Archivo** — the wordmark artwork only; use the supplied vector, never retype "Qatom" in a system font.
- Money: three decimals, tabular — `$0.001`, `$4.500`.

## Logo & the mark's meaning
The mark = three parts in one motion:
- **The circle — the fortress:** the deterministic console holding assets and wallets, sovereign and verifiable.
- **The travelling piece — the proxy:** the payout / split / agent twin that transacts across the wider ecosystem.
- **The strike — the transaction:** value settling in one decisive pass. Its angle is **−40.66°** — the brand's diagonal everywhere.
One colour only, never two-tone. Approved: white, white-on-verdigris (signature), pine, oxide. Never stretch, rotate, skew, recolour off-palette, add shadow/glow/gradient/outline, or crowd its clear space (≥ 0.5 × mark height).

## Graphic device — "the strike, extended"
The mark's strike pulled out as a line at −40.66°. Belongs to the mark; one per surface; verdigris on neutral/deep; never boxed, over photography, or duplicated.

## Voice
- **"Analytically bold. Refreshingly plain."** Compress the consequence, not the science.
- Balance every asset on **Digital ⟷ Organic** — the apex of the digital and the organic; never at an extreme.
- Always: *integrity is portable · verified, not trusted · you keep 100% · making humans stronger.*
- Never: *trust us · revolutionary / disrupting · Web3 or token hype · "unlock the future" · buzzword stacks.* No code/CLI in brand copy.

## Layout & finish
Generous spacing (let elements breathe). Frosted-glass cards, hairline borders, crisp finishes, pill buttons (verdigris fill = primary; near-black or white ghost). Single-column grid, wide margins. Energetic and innovative, yet enterprise-congruent. Motion: the 260ms Strike — one decisive token.

## Nomenclature & spelling
Product names: Qatom Skill, Qatom Twin, Qatom Proxy, Qatom Binders, Qatom Managed, Qatom Enterprise VPC. Always write **"Qatom"** — never all-caps in body.

Brand guideline · Lockup construction

One lockup, built to a rule.

The horizontal lockup is a fixed relationship, not an arrangement. The mark, the space, the wordmark, and the colour are locked together. Reproduce it from these ratios and it stays consistent and elevated at any size, on any surface.

Proportion and spacing

The mark sets the unit.

Let M be the height of the mark. The mark is square, one M tall and one M wide. Everything else is measured from it, so the lockup can never be eyeballed.

MeasureValueIn practice
Mark1 M × 1 MSquare. The unit for the whole lockup.
Wordmark cap height0.73 MThe caps of Qatom are just under three quarters of the mark height. The mark reads a touch taller so the circle balances the type.
Gap, mark to wordmark0.20 MOne fifth of the mark. Never tighter, never looser.
Vertical alignmentshared centreAlign on the optical centre axis, not the type baseline.
Clear space0.5 MKeep at least half the mark height clear on all four sides. Nothing enters this zone.
Lockup ratio~4.08 : 1Overall width to height, when set correctly.

Colour

One lockup, one colour.

The mark and the wordmark are always the same single colour. Never two tone, never a coloured mark beside black type. The lockup reproduces in exactly four colours, each chosen for a job.

White · #FFFFFFThe default on dark grounds and photography. The most used.
Verdigris · #33BAA6The signature, on dark grounds. Colour as identity.
Pine · #0C1A17The brand black, on white and light neutrals. Cool, never pure black.
Oxide · #1C4B4BA quieter deep teal for light grounds, where pine feels too hard.

Discipline

Do and don't.

Do

  • Build from M every time: 0.73 M caps, 0.20 M gap, 0.5 M clear space.
  • Keep the whole lockup in one approved colour.
  • Align mark and wordmark on the shared centre axis.
  • Scale the lockup as one locked group.
  • Drop to the mark alone when space is tight, rather than shrinking the lockup past its floor.

Don't

  • Two tone it, a verdigris mark with black type is not the lockup.
  • Change the gap, the cap ratio, or the alignment by eye.
  • Stretch, condense, rotate, or re-space the wordmark.
  • Add shadows, outlines, gradients, or glows.
  • Place a low-contrast colour on a low-contrast ground, or crowd the clear space.

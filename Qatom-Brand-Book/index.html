SECTION 04
Colour.

Neutral carries it. Verdigris signs it.

Philosophy

A mineral palette, moving the eye.

Neutral grounds do the work — near-black and a cool near-white carry every surface, so the eye has somewhere quiet to rest. The teal family then does the moving: verdigris, mist, oxide and pine highlight, vary, and lead the eye across a spread. Verdigris stays the signature; the others add depth without shouting over it.

Why teal: a mineral colour that joins sky and water — an organic anchor, a note of permanence. It reads as the patina copper and bronze earn as they oxidise: exposed, tested, proven over time. Soothing and comforting, yet energetic and alive. Integrity, built into every asset.

Three registers carry it: deep tech (systems, compute, code), people & commerce (the humans and teams who transact), and a restrained elemental note (sky and water — the green-compute conscience). We are neither a wellness brand nor National Geographic; nature is the accent, not the subject.

72MOTION · THE STRIKE
The signature device

The strike, extended.

The device is the mark's own strike — the transaction — pulled out as a line and drawn across a surface. The arc echoes the travelling piece: the payout, the split, the proxy moving through the wider ecosystem.

It always runs at the mark's angle and belongs to the mark — never a free diagonal. One strike per surface, for hero and section moments. Verdigris on a neutral or deep ground; never boxed, never over photography, never duplicated.
